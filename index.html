Target (4% rule)

Target corpus uses the 4% rule: annual future expenses ÷ 0.04, with expenses inflated at the rate set above. Before retirement, surplus cash (after EPF and expenses) is reinvested each year. From retirement age, the model withdraws that year's inflation-adjusted expenses from EPF, FD and investments proportionally, while the remainder keeps earning its own rate, through to the "project until" age. This is a planning estimate, not financial advice.
